New to Grappling? Start Here

Nearly everyone who walks through our door has never grappled before. Here is exactly what happens, what to bring, and the answer to every question people are too polite to ask.

You do not need to be fit, strong or experienced

This is the single most common worry, and it stops more people than anything else. You do not need to get in shape first. You do not need any martial arts background. Most of our members walked in with neither, and the ones who have been here longest were beginners not that long ago.

Grappling is built on technique and leverage rather than size and strength, which is exactly why it works for people who are not the biggest or the fittest in the room. You set your own pace from your first session, and nobody is going to throw you in at the deep end.

What happens on your first visit

  1. Claim your free trial online Fill in your details and we email you a QR code to sign in with. Nothing to pay, and no card needed.
  2. Arrive ten minutes early That gives us time to get your paperwork signed and show you around the place before anything starts.
  3. Get a tour of the facilities Mats, changing areas, the strength and conditioning room. You will know where everything is before you step on.
  4. Train You join the 6pm class like anyone else. A coach will keep an eye on you and pair you with someone who will look after you.
  5. Stay for open mat, or do not Open mat runs 7–8pm straight after. Stay if you are enjoying it, head home if one hour was plenty. Both are completely normal.

What to wear and bring

  • Shorts and a t-shirt — anything you can move in. No special kit needed for your first session.
  • A water bottle — you will want it.
  • Flip flops or sliders — for walking around off the mats. This one matters more than people expect: mat hygiene depends on it.

Once you are training regularly we have custom Forged Grappling rashguards you can buy, but there is no rush and no obligation.

Is it going to hurt?

There is no striking in grappling — nobody is going to punch you. The sport is about positional control, takedowns, and submissions through joint locks and chokes, and every one of those has a built-in off switch: you tap, your partner lets go, you reset and go again.

Beginners learn how to move, how to fall safely and how to tap early before anything else. Coaches stay on the mat throughout, and the culture here is built around controlled, intelligent rolling rather than winning a training round. You will be sore in places you did not expect. You should not be hurt.

Grappling for women

Women train and compete at every level of this sport, for the same reason grappling suits anyone who is not the biggest person in the room: technique and leverage beat size. You always choose who you partner with, rounds are controlled, and you set your own pace from day one.

How old do you need to be?

Our classes are for ages 16 and up. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian just needs to sign your waiver on your first visit. We do not currently run a dedicated kids’ class.
