No-Gi Grappling Classes in Westbury

Coached classes five nights a week — heavy on wrestling and takedowns — with open mat straight after and a free community session every Friday. All levels train in the same room, beginners included.

The weekly timetable

We run five nights a week, Monday to Friday. Every evening opens with a coached class at 6pm and rolls straight into open mat at 7pm, so you can train for one hour or two depending on what your week allows.

No-Gi Grappling — Mon to Fri, 6–7pm

The coached class, and the one to come to if you are starting out. It is open to every level: beginners and experienced grapplers train in the same room, and the coaching is adjusted to whoever is in front of us.

Every class follows the same three-part shape, so you always know what you are walking into:

  • Games and dynamic drills to warm up — movement first, so you are ready before anything technical starts.
  • Technique for problem solving — we explain the why behind the position, not just the steps, then you drill it with a partner.
  • Positional sparring to finish — you pressure test what you just learned in a controlled round, at a pace you set.

Structured Open Mat — Mon to Thu, 7–8pm

Supervised mat time straight after the coached class. Spar, drill something you are stuck on, or pull a coach aside and ask. It is not a free-for-all: coaches stay on the mat and there is guidance available the whole hour, which is what makes it useful rather than just extra rounds.

This is where most of the actual improvement happens. Class gives you the tool; open mat is where you find out when it works.

Community Open Mat — Friday, 7–8pm

Free and open to everyone, whether you train with us or not. Bring friends from other local clubs, roll with people you do not usually see, and ask the coaches anything. If you want to get a feel for the room before committing to anything, this is the easiest door to walk through.

Wrestling and takedowns

A large part of what we actually teach is wrestling. Jack and Harry’s sessions in particular are built around it: how to hold a stance, change levels, get to a takedown and finish on top — and how to get back to your feet when someone does it to you.

We still call the session “no-gi grappling” rather than “wrestling class”, and that is deliberate. Labels put people in boxes, and plenty of our members came in thinking they were only interested in one part of the sport. You get the takedowns either way.

So if you have been searching for wrestling in Westbury or the surrounding towns and finding nothing: this is it. There is no separate wrestling class to sign up for — it is already in the 6pm session, five nights a week.

Strength and conditioning

Every membership includes access to our S&C area — power rack, rower and air bike — so you can train around your grappling in the same building rather than paying for a second gym.

What to bring

Shorts and a t-shirt, a water bottle, and flip flops or sliders for walking around off the mats. That is genuinely it for your first session. There is a fuller rundown of what to expect on the beginners page.
