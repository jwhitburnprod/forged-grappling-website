The Coaches

The people you will actually be training with, five nights a week — on the mat, not watching from the side.

Four coaches, five nights a week, all of them on the mat rather than watching from the side. Between them they cover most of what grappling has to offer — competition experience, leg locks and catch wrestling, guard and passing, and the patience to get a complete beginner started.

Mitch Cullum

Coach · 5 years training

Mitch brings years of competition experience to the mat, and it shows in how he coaches — he has been in the positions he is teaching, under pressure, against people trying very hard to stop him.

His own game is built around slick guard play and a passing game that gives everyone in the room problems, whatever their level. If you have ever wondered why your guard keeps getting flattened, he is the one to ask.

Jack Whitburn

Coach · 4 years training

Jack’s background is in coaching athletes, and he approaches grappling the same way: work out what an individual actually needs, then build the session around getting them there.

That focus on maximising individual performance means the same class can look different depending on who is in it. If you have a specific goal — a competition, a problem position, getting back on the mats after time off — tell him and he will build around it.

Nathan Cullum

Coach · 3 years training

Nathan has a knack for breaking techniques down so they actually click for newer grapplers — not just the steps, but why each one matters and what happens when you skip it.

He is particularly good at helping people piece together a complete game without holes, which is the difference between knowing a lot of moves and actually being hard to beat. If you are early on, you will spend a lot of useful time with him.

Harry Whitburn

Coach · 3 years training

Harry competes at international level and has the record to match, built on an unconventional style rooted in catch wrestling rather than the more common competition template.

He is a master of leg locks, and the entries he teaches tend to be the ones nobody in the room has seen before. Expect to leave his sessions with something you have never been shown anywhere else.

Train with them
