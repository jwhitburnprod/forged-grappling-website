We open for the 6pm class and close after open mat at 8pm, Monday to Friday. If you are coming for a trial session, aim to arrive by 5:50pm so there is time for paperwork and a tour. Full timetable on the classes page.

Getting here

We are based on the Woodlands Industrial Estate on Eden Vale Road in Westbury, which puts us within an easy drive of Trowbridge, Warminster, Frome and Bradford on Avon. Members travel in from across Wiltshire and over the Somerset border.

Westbury railway station is in the same town, so the gym is reachable by train as well as car.

Questions before you come?

Email info@forgedgrappling.co.uk or call 07463 580506. If your question is about what to expect on the night, the beginners page probably answers it already.
