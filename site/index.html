WHAT IS NO-GI GRAPPLING?

No-gi grappling is a combat sport that uses takedowns, positional control, chokes and joint locks to control and submit your opponent. Unlike MMA, there is no striking and we don’t wear the heavy kimonos seen in traditional BJJ. It’s physical, technical, surprisingly addictive, and far more accessible than most people expect.

WHY FORGED

Built for No-Gi Grappling

Forged was built specifically for no-gi grappling by coaches who love the sport. Every session, every drill, every coaching decision is made with one goal in mind: making you a better grappler.

Developing Effective Skills

Our classes are structured to build skills that actually work under pressure. We explain the why behind every technique, then give you the chance to practice and pressure test it live.

A Club That Looks After You

Whatever your experience level, you’ll be welcomed, looked after, and able to train at your own pace. The culture here is built around people enjoying their training, whether it’s your first day or your thousandth.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

None at all. We have plenty of beginners at Forged and our 6pm classes give you everything you need to start training. We ease you in and make sure you know what you’re doing at every step.
Just wear some shorts and a t-shirt for your first session. Bring a water bottle and some flip flops or sliders so you can walk around off the mats. Once you’re training regularly, we have custom Forged Grappling rashguards available that you can buy.
Grappling focuses on positional control, takedowns, and submissions through joint locks and chokes. No striking. Member safety is our top priority: beginners learn to move, fall, and work with a partner safely from day one. Coaches stay on the mat and the culture is built around controlled, intelligent rolling.
Our 6pm taught classes always follow the same structure so you know what to expect. We start with some games and dynamic drills to get you warmed up, followed by specific techniques for problem solving, then finishing with positional sparring.
