Which option suits you?

Just visiting, or want to try a few sessions? The Visitors Pass is £15 for a day. If you know you will come back, the Multiple Visit Pass brings five sessions down to £60.

Training regularly? Membership is £80 a month with unlimited training, so it works out cheaper once you train more than a handful of times a month. It also includes the members community, video library and a monthly guest pass.
