No contracts

Monthly memberships run month to month and you can cancel any time after the first 30 days — it takes effect at the end of the billing month. There is no joining fee and no minimum term. Annual memberships are paid up front for twelve months and work out at £66.67 a month, which is twelve months for the price of ten.

Which membership suits you

  • Training two or more nights a week? Unlimited is the one — at two sessions a week it already costs less per session than the Lite membership.
  • One night is all your week allows? Lite keeps you on the mats without paying for time you cannot use.
  • Passing through, or a member elsewhere? The visitor pass gets you a full evening — class and open mat — for £15.

You are not locked in either way. Plenty of members start on Lite and move up once training becomes a habit. Just tell a coach and we will sort it.

Free every Friday

Our Community Open Mat on Friday evenings is free and open to everyone, member or not. No pass needed — just turn up.

Full details of billing, cancellation and the guarantee are in our terms and conditions.
