3. Memberships and pricing

3.1 Visitors Pass

A Visitors Pass is available for £15 per day and provides two hours of grappling plus full access to the S&C area for that day. Visitors Passes are non-refundable except as required by law.

3.2 Forged Membership

Forged Membership is £80 per month and provides full access to everything we offer at Forged, including unlimited training, the members community and video library, and seminar discounts. This rate is subject to change. We will give you no less than 30 days' written notice of any price increase.

3.3 Multiple Visit Pass

A Multiple Visit Pass bundles five Visitors Pass sessions together for a discounted rate of £60. Sessions may be used across multiple visits and do not expire. Multiple Visit Passes are non-refundable except as required by law.

3.4 Trial pass

A free trial pass is available for first-time visitors only, providing access for a single session. Only one free trial pass may be used per person.

An existing member may earn this credit once for each different new member they refer; a new member may only redeem this offer once, as the person being referred. Credit is not exchangeable for cash and is non-transferable.
