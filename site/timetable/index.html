What each session is

No-Gi Grappling Mon–Fri · 6–7pm

Taught class for all levels with technical instruction, task-based games and live sparring. A structured session designed to develop new skills and sharpen existing ones. This is the class most new starters begin with.

Structured Open Mat Mon–Thu · 7–8pm

Supervised mat time to spar, drill or ask the coaches questions. Guided support to develop your own game.

Community Open Mat Fri · 7–8pm

Always free and open to the whole community. Bring friends from local clubs for some rolls and ask the coaches anything.

Which session should I come to?

If you have never trained before, start with the 6pm no-gi grappling class. Beginners are welcome at every class, and new starters are usually paired with experienced partners. Not sure what to expect? Read what happens in your free trial or our guide to what no-gi grappling is.

Prices for classes and open mats are on our prices page.
