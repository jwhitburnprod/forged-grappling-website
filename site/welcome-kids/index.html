What your child will learn first

We keep it simple and we keep it fun. In the first few weeks they will learn:

  • How to move around on the mats and get back up safely
  • How to fall without getting hurt
  • A few basic positions, and how to get out of them
  • How to train with a partner: taking turns, being kind and tapping when they need to
  • How to listen to the coach and follow what the group is doing

How the first few months go

Do not worry about how good they are. Turning up and getting comfortable is the job.

  1. First week

    Some children walk in and get stuck straight in. Others hold on to your leg. Both are fine. They are learning how class works and who everyone is.

  2. First month

    They know the routine, they know the coaches and they start to make friends. Moves that felt strange begin to feel familiar. Regular attendance is what makes this happen.

  3. Three months

    They are confident on the mats and they look forward to class. Techniques start to link together, and you will probably notice more confidence outside the gym too.
