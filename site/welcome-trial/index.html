How to use your [DEAL_LENGTH_TEXT]

Here is the order we suggest.

  1. Taught class first

    Start with the 6pm No-Gi Grappling class. A coach teaches a technique, you practise it with a partner, and everyone in the room is there to help you learn.

  2. Then add an open mat

    Once you have been to a taught class, try a structured open mat. You get supervised mat time to practise what you were shown and ask the coaches questions.

  3. Friday for the community

    The Friday community open mat is a relaxed way to meet more people and see how everyone trains.

Tips for the first week

  • Arrive 10 minutes early so you are not rushing
  • Bring shorts, a rashguard or fitted t-shirt, and a bottle of water
  • No jewellery, and keep your nails short
  • Ask questions. Nobody minds, and everyone has asked the same ones
  • Tap whenever you need to. There is nothing to prove
  • Do not try to remember everything. Pick up one or two things each session
