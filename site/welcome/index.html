How the first few months go

Do not worry about being good. Turn up regularly and get comfortable. The rest follows.

  1. First week

    Expect to feel a bit lost. Everyone does. Learn how class runs, get to know a few people and ask the coach anything. You can tap whenever you need to, so there is nothing to prove.

  2. First month

    Names start to stick and the moves stop feeling foreign. You will begin to recognise positions in class and get tired a little less. Find a rhythm that fits your week and stick to it.

  3. Three months

    You know your way around the mats and you feel at home. Techniques start to link together, and you will notice yourself reacting instead of freezing. Fitness, confidence and friendships tend to show up around now.

Sessions you can keep up every week will get you further than one big push.
