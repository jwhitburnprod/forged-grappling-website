Your free grappling trial in Westbury

Your first session at Forged Grappling costs nothing, and you do not need any experience. Our no-gi classes run Monday to Friday at 6pm at Unit 21, Woodlands Industrial Estate in Westbury, a short drive from Trowbridge, Warminster and Frome.

How your first session works

  1. Claim your trial onlineUse any of the buttons on this page, fill in your details and we will email you a QR code to sign in with.
  2. Arrive 10 minutes earlyThis gives us time to get your paperwork signed, show you around and introduce you to the coaches before class starts.
  3. Warm up and learnClass starts with movement-based drills, then the coach teaches a technique and you practise it with a partner. As a beginner you will usually be paired with an experienced training partner.
  4. Try some live practiceThe end of class is positional sparring at an intensity matched to your level. You can tap at any time, and nobody expects you to know what you are doing yet.

What to bring

Bring

  • Shorts and a t-shirt
  • A water bottle
  • Flip flops or sliders for off the mats
  • Your QR code from the confirmation email

Leave at home

  • Anything with zips, buttons or pockets
  • A gi: this is a no-gi gym, so you do not need one
  • Any worry about being fit enough or experienced enough

Common questions

Do I need to be fit? No. Fitness comes from training, and you can rest whenever you need to.

Will I be the only beginner? Unlikely. Plenty of our members started recently and remember exactly how it feels.

What happens after the trial? There is no pressure. If you enjoy it, see our prices, including a 30-day guarantee, and the class timetable.
